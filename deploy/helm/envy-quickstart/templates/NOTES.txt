Envy quickstart is installed with Istio, PostgreSQL, and the Tea shop baseline.

Open the dashboard and preview ingress with one port-forward:
  kubectl -n envy-quickstart port-forward service/envy-ingress 8080:80

Dashboard: http://127.0.0.1:8080 (admin / admin)
Baseline:  http://shop.envy.localhost:8080/products

In the dashboard, choose Tea shop / staging, override pricing with:
  {{ .Values.sample.repository }}:{{ .Values.sample.previewTag }}
Create the preview, wait for readiness, then open its URL with /products.
The preview price is 990; the baseline remains 1200.

CLI users: ENVY_API_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8080
No CLI is needed to use the dashboard. This installation is for local evaluation.

Before uninstalling, destroy all previews in the dashboard.
  helm uninstall envy -n envy-quickstart
PostgreSQL's volume and credentials are retained. Istio CRDs also remain.
For a complete reset, delete your dedicated local cluster.
